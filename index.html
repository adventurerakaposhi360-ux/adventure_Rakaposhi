Wildlife Conservation Expeditions

Trophy Hunting in Gilgit-Baltistan

Gilgit-Baltistan hosts an internationally acclaimed community-managed trophy hunting program where official auction proceeds directly empower local wildlife conservation and high-altitude mountain village development.

Under strictly regulated annual government quotas, licensed hunting permits are auctioned for world-renowned alpine game including the iconic Astore Markhor, high-altitude Himalayan Ibex, and rare Blue Sheep (Bharal).

Premier National Trophy

Astore Markhor

Pakistan's national animal, famed worldwide for its majestic flared spiral horns. Hunted under strict community-protected quotas across designated reserves in Gilgit, Astore, and Rondu.

High-Altitude Alpine Crags

Himalayan Ibex

Renowned for imposing ribbed scimitar horns reaching exceptional lengths. Thrives on precipitous cliffs across Hunza, Nagar, Skardu, Ghizer, and Diamer districts.

Remote Karakoram Rarity

Blue Sheep (Bharal)

An elusive, agile high-elevation species inhabiting windswept moraines and scree slopes in Shimshal and Karakoram National Park, offering a world-class mountain expedition.

80% of auction revenue goes directly to local mountain communities for schools, health clinics, and wildlife game guards.
